# Supplier Activation Policy

- **Document owner:** Head of Procurement Operations
- **Review cycle:** Annual
- **Scope:** New API-enabled suppliers paid through the Billing Platform

Supplier activation contains three distinct control domains. Compliance owns
legal-entity and risk evidence in GRC Hub. Integration Engineering owns the
technical workspace and connectivity evidence in the Integration Console.
Finance Operations owns settlement authorization in the Billing Platform.

A hand-over permits the next domain to begin but does not transfer ownership of
the preceding decision. Adjacency in the end-to-end route is therefore not, by
itself, evidence that actions belong to one business stage.
