# Supplier Settlement Standard

- **Procedure owner:** Finance Specialist
- **Used in:** Billing Platform
- **Entry condition:** Integration Engineering returned a successful connectivity test

## Activate supplier settlements

1. Activate settlement account in the Billing Platform.
2. Activate settlement mandate in the Billing Platform.
3. Activate settlement schedule in the Billing Platform.

The supplier is active only after a second Finance Specialist approves the
account, mandate and schedule under the maker-checker control.
