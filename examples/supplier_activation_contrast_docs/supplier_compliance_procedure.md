# Supplier Compliance Procedure

- **Procedure owner:** Compliance Analyst
- **Used in:** GRC Hub
- **Entry condition:** Supplier Operations submitted a complete registration package

## Assess the supplier

1. Assess vendor registration in GRC Hub.
2. Assess vendor identity in GRC Hub.
3. Assess vendor compliance in GRC Hub.

The approved compliance decision is the formal hand-over to Integration
Engineering. A rejected or unresolved assessment does not enter the technical
stage.
