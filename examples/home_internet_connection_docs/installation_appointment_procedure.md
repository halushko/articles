# Installation Appointment Procedure

- **Procedure reference:** HIF-SCH-003
- **Procedure owner:** Field Service Scheduling
- **Used in:** Workforce Scheduling Platform
- **Entry condition:** The order is serviceable and the customer agreement is active

## Review scheduling constraints

Read the serviceability notes, required technician skill, estimated work
duration and any building access window. Confirm that required network capacity
and customer equipment remain reserved for the order.

## Offer an appointment

Search the Workforce Scheduling Platform for appointment windows that satisfy
the job constraints. Offer the customer at least two available windows when the
capacity plan allows it. Explain the expected duration and any preparation the
customer must complete.

## Confirm attendance and access

Record the window selected by the customer. Confirm that an adult will be
present, the technician can reach the communications room when necessary, and
the proposed gateway location has power.

Reserve the field slot and send the appointment confirmation with the order
reference and arrival window.

## Reconfirm before dispatch

Send a reminder through the approved channel before the visit. Ask the customer
to report access changes or a need to reschedule as early as possible.

If the customer cancels before dispatch, release the field slot and arrange a
new appointment. Otherwise keep the confirmed visit and make its access notes
available to the assigned technician.
