# Customer Identity and Service Agreement Procedure

- **Procedure reference:** HIF-SAL-006
- **Procedure owner:** Residential Sales Operations
- **Used in:** Customer and Contract Platform
- **Entry condition:** The premises has a serviceable result

## Confirm the contracting customer

Ask the customer for the identity and contact information required by the local
contracting rules. Compare those details with the order and verify that the
customer is old enough to enter the agreement.

Record the verification method and result without copying unnecessary identity
document images into free-text notes. Stop the contract step and request a
compliant verification when the available evidence is insufficient.

## Confirm the offer

Review the available tariff, minimum term, expected access speed, recurring
charge and one-time installation charges with the customer. Confirm whether the
provider supplies or rents the home gateway and whether optional services have
been requested.

Remove any option that the customer did not explicitly accept. Record the final
offer version in the Customer and Contract Platform.

## Create and issue the agreement

Generate the service agreement with the verified customer, premises, tariff and
equipment details. Send the agreement and the required pre-contract information
through the customer's approved communication channel.

Give the customer an opportunity to review the documents before requesting
acceptance. Do not treat delivery of an email or text message as acceptance.

## Record acceptance

Capture the customer's electronic signature or another approved acceptance
event. Store its timestamp, channel and agreement version in the contract
record. Link the resulting contract identifier to the original order.

Return the selected tariff, equipment choice, accepted charges and contract
identifier to Order Management.
