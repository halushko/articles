# Home Internet Order Fulfilment Guide

- **Procedure reference:** HIF-ORD-001
- **Procedure owner:** Residential Order Management
- **Used in:** Order Management Portal
- **Applies to:** New fixed-broadband service for a residential customer

## Purpose

This guide coordinates a home internet order from the customer's first request
until the service is active and billing can begin. It applies to apartments and
houses served by the provider's fixed access network. A relocation of an active
service and a repair of an existing connection follow separate procedures.

The order record is the common reference for sales, network planning, field
operations and billing. Each team records its result in that record rather than
starting an unrelated request.

## Receive and register the request

A sales adviser receives the request through the website, contact centre or a
retail store. Confirm the installation address, the customer's contact details,
the preferred service plan and whether the customer rents or owns the premises.

Search the Order Management Portal for another open order at the same address.
Update a matching draft when it belongs to the same customer; otherwise create a
new order and issue its reference to the customer.

## Complete the serviceability review

Continue with the *Premises Serviceability and Building Access Procedure*.
Record the returned access technology, available capacity, estimated lead time
and any landlord or building-manager dependency in the order.

Do not promise an installation date while the address remains blocked or its
network route has not been confirmed.

## Prepare the customer agreement

Continue with the *Customer Identity and Service Agreement Procedure*. The
signed agreement, selected tariff, equipment choice and installation charges
must be attached to the same order before scheduling begins.

## Arrange the visit

Continue with the *Installation Appointment Procedure*. Record the confirmed
appointment window, access instructions and the name of the adult who will meet
the technician.

## Complete the premises work

Continue with the *Residential Field Installation Runbook*. Keep the order in
the installation stage until the technician returns either a completed job or a
specific blocker with supporting evidence.

## Activate the connection

Continue with the *Service Activation and Line Test Runbook*. Store the service
identifier, assigned equipment identifiers and the technical test result in the
order.

## Confirm service and close the order

Continue with the *Service Acceptance, Billing and Order Closure Standard*.
The order is complete only after the customer-facing check, billing start date
and final status have been recorded.

## Related policy

The *Residential Broadband Fulfilment Policy* defines service promises,
responsibilities and evidence-retention rules for this process.
