# Residential Broadband Fulfilment Policy

- **Policy reference:** HIF-POL-001
- **Document owner:** Residential Service Operations
- **Audience:** Sales, Network Planning, Field Operations, Activation, Billing
- **Review cycle:** Annual or after a material change to the access network

## Purpose and scope

This policy defines the controls for supplying a new fixed-broadband connection
to a residential customer. It applies from receipt of an order until the
customer can use the service and the accepted billing date is recorded.

Fault repair, relocation of an existing service and construction of a new
access network are outside this process. Those activities may be raised as
linked work when they block an order, but they retain their own ownership and
approval rules.

## Customer commitments

Dates and speeds communicated to a customer must be supported by the current
serviceability result. A sales channel may explain an indicative lead time, but
it must not present an appointment as confirmed before field capacity and
premises access are reserved.

The customer must receive the tariff, minimum term, recurring charges,
installation charges and supplied-equipment terms before accepting the service
agreement.

## Operational ownership

Order Management owns the end-to-end order and customer status. Network
Planning owns address serviceability and shared-building access constraints.
Field Operations owns work at the premises. Activation Operations owns logical
provisioning and network tests. Customer Operations owns service acceptance,
billing release and closure.

A hand-off changes responsibility for a technical task but does not create a
new customer order. Every task and fault must retain the original order
reference.

## Evidence and privacy

Teams must retain the evidence needed to explain serviceability, customer
acceptance, physical installation, network activation and the billing start
date. Access to identity records, building permissions and premises photographs
must follow the provider's privacy and retention rules.

Passwords, Wi-Fi passphrases and complete payment credentials must not be copied
into order notes. Diagnostic values should be limited to what another operator
needs to verify the result or continue the work.

## Completion rule

An installation is not complete merely because a technician closed a field
job, and an order is not complete merely because a provisioning command
returned success. Billing may start only after the service has an active
technical result and the customer-visible acceptance requirement has been met.
